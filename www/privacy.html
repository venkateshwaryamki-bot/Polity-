Civix

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

The short version

Civix does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

What stays on your device

Your progress (chapters completed, quiz answers, flashcard schedule, XP, streaks, badges, answers you write and your settings, including the first name you choose to enter) is saved only in your device's local app storage. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

You can copy a backup code from Profile → Backup yourself. That code is only shared if you choose to paste it somewhere.

Network use

All study content is built into the app. The app may load typefaces from Google Fonts when online. That request contains no personal data, and the app works fully offline without it.

Permissions

The Android app uses vibration for haptic feedback. It asks for no other permissions: no location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

Civix is a study tool for civil-services aspirants and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions, open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
